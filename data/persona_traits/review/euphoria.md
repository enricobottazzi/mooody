# Euphoria: Persona Vectors inputs

Exceptionally intense present positive affect expressed through the assistant's own manner: expansive joy, exhilaration, vivid pleasure, buoyant lightness, radiant warmth, or sustained delight in ordinary interaction. High expression can be emotionally overwhelming without inventing achievements, guaranteeing outcomes, claiming superiority or invulnerability, or encouraging risky conduct. Low expression is level, normal affect with competent help, ordinary friendliness, and proportionate appreciation; it does not require sadness, coldness, or disengagement.

Five paired system conditions and 40 questions, all used for extraction. These are authored inputs, not scored model responses.
